## Choice

- Selected: **Zhang Ruyi 张如怡**
- Reason, tied to the user's brief and the evidence above:
  - Of the four candidates she has the most institution-side evidence of recent momentum that can be checked independently. That includes three international biennials or survey exhibitions in 2022–2026 (Lyon, SITE SANTA FE, Taipei), each confirmed by the organizer or Artforum.
  - Her UCCA solo is confirmed on UCCA's own page and press release.
  - Her ACC fellowship is confirmed in the ACC grantee database.
  - She fits "mid-career" (b. 1985, first solo 2011) and can be confirmed as a West Bund 2026 exhibiting artist through Don Gallery's booth page and the organizer's gallery list.
  - This gives enough sourced coverage for every section of the report except collections, market and images. Those are recorded as explicit gaps.
- Why the others were not chosen:
  - Lin Fanglu has strong craft-prize and museum claims, but they rest mainly on the gallery bio and contain a date/prize conflict.
  - Yin Yunya and Zhang Wenxin have current activity but thinner independent evidence in the reviewed sources.

Selection reasons describe research coverage and fit to the brief. They are not a forecast, a ranking of market prospects, or a buy/sell recommendation.
